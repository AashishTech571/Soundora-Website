SOUNDORA Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Welcome to SOUNDORA. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how SOUNDORA handles information when you use our music application and website.

1. Guest Access

SOUNDORA provides Guest access. Users do not need to create an account or register using their name, email address or phone number to use the app.

The app may use a Guest ID to identify a guest session and support app functionality.

2. Personal Information

SOUNDORA does not ask users to provide a name, email address or phone number for Guest access.

3. Device and App Information

Information may be processed on your device to support app features and improve the user experience. The exact information depends on the features you use.

4. Third-Party Services

SOUNDORA may use third-party services in the future. Such services may process information according to their own privacy policies. This policy will be updated when such services are introduced.

5. Children's Privacy

SOUNDORA does not knowingly ask users to submit personal information to create a Guest account.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features or data practices change. Updated information will be published on this page.

7. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact information provided on the official SOUNDORA website.
